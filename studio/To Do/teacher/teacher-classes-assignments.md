# Teacher Classes, Homework & Parent Notifications

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** teacher

## Goal

Build teacher-facing class management capabilities in stages.

## Intended scope

- Create/manage classes and associated learners with role-based access.
- Assign homework and quizzes, track submission and attendance with appropriate sensitivity.
- Send class announcements and parent notifications, including opt-in automated was-here/was-not-here or completed/not-completed notices.
- Protect student privacy and avoid false automated absence/performance accusations.

## Open decisions and constraints

Scope controls, parental rights, regional requirements and teacher staffing workflows.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
