# Teacher Ecosystem Discovery Research

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** teacher

## Goal

Study teachers' actual pain points before building a large teacher suite.

## Intended scope

- Interview teachers in varied class sizes and settings and validate workflows for assessments, attendance, feedback and parent contact.
- Prioritize reducing administrative burden, including possible automated checking of student notes where pedagogically/legally appropriate.
- Document accessibility, curriculum/region differences, student privacy, teacher autonomy and time saved.

## Open decisions and constraints

Define research cohort, validation criteria and roles before implementation.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
