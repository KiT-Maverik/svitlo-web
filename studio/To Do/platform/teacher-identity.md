# Teacher Identity & Relationship to Accounts

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** platform

## Goal

Determine teacher account structure before implementing school-facing access.

## Intended scope

- Consider teacher as role of an existing account versus a separate teacher identity/portal.
- Allow future class management, assignments, review and access controls.
- Preserve family/child account privacy and distinct institutional permissions.

## Open decisions and constraints

Decision remains open: standalone teacher account or attached teacher profile; involve real teacher research.

**Implementation note:** This is a product-backlog specification, not approval to edit runtime contracts, add dependencies or claim the feature exists.
