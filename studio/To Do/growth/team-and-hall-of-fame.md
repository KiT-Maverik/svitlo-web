# Team Page & Contributor Hall of Fame

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** growth

## Goal

Thank verified contributors to the project.

## Intended scope

- Offer public team page and opt-in Hall of Fame for monetary donors, useful issue reports, code/translation contributions and other meaningful support.
- Get permission before naming contributors; allow anonymity, name removal and privacy controls.
- Avoid publishing children's personal data or sensitive donation histories.

## Open decisions and constraints

Define attribution thresholds, verification and moderation.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
