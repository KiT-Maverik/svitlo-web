# Ukraine Scholarships & Eligibility

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** growth

## Goal

Offer subsidized/free access to eligible Ukrainian families and service-connected groups.

## Intended scope

- Candidate groups from brief: orphans, large families, serving/veteran/wounded military and affected families, POW families, families of fallen servicemembers, emergency responders/firefighters and other approved categories.
- **Required part of the Ukraine-scholarship application flow (product requirement):** show a field labeled **«Слава Україні»** and require the applicant to enter **«Героям слава»**, without displaying the expected response as a hint. This is a proposed application interaction, not a reliable identity or eligibility check.
- Independently validate actual scholarship eligibility using legitimate, privacy-respecting evidence. The phrase response alone must **never** be treated as proof of citizenship, military/family status, identity or entitlement; the legal and accessibility implications of making it a submission requirement must be reviewed before launch.
- Never collect sensitive war/family status without a clear lawful purpose, protection and retention policy.

## Candidate scholarship programme names

**Status:** Naming concepts for editorial review, **not approved eligibility rules or launched programmes**. The names should express gratitude, solidarity and opportunity without implying that a child must prove a family's merits to deserve learning.

| Proposed public name (UA) | Intended audience / narrative | Decision status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **«Дякую за захист»** | Children of serving military personnel, veterans, wounded defenders and families of fallen, missing or captured servicemembers, subject to eligibility review | User-proposed name |
| **«Дякую за освіту»** | Proposed gratitude-themed name; may naturally fit children from educators' families. **Clarify audience before approval:** the user's original list mentioned rescue and utility professions alongside this title | User-proposed name; matching to audience requires review |
| **«Дякую за порятунок»** | Children of emergency responders, firefighters and medical rescuers; acknowledges work protecting lives | Editorial naming suggestion |
| **«Дякую за світло»** | Children of electricians, energy workers and other critical infrastructure workers who restore essential services | Editorial naming suggestion |
| **«Велика родина»** | Children who are orphans, deprived of parental care, or raised in one-parent families. **The name means a wider community that cares about and supports the child, not a household with many relatives.** | User-proposed name; emotional purpose clarified |
| **«Рукавичка»** | Children from **large families**; a playful reference to the Ukrainian fairy tale where many characters find room together in one mitten — warmth, belonging and mutual support | User-proposed playful candidate for review |

**Emotional promise of «Велика родина»:** a child should feel **«Ти не сам/сама. Є люди, яким ти небайдужий/небайдужа, які готові підтримати тебе в навчанні»**. This refers to the larger Svitlo community of caring people, not the child's family size or marital status of their parents. The tone should be warm, dignified and non-pitying; avoid making an unfunded promise of unconditional lifelong assistance.

**«Рукавичка» naming rationale:** the Ukrainian folk-tale image is memorable and affectionate: even when there are many of us, there is a place for everyone. Consider an optional illustrated mitten motif. It is a **playful working name**, not a final brand decision. Previously suggested «Багато сердець», «Сила великої родини» and «Більше можливостей» remain alternatives for editorial comparison.

**Naming consistency:** «Дякую за захист», «Дякую за освіту», «Дякую за порятунок» and «Дякую за світло» can form one gratitude-themed family of programmes; «Велика родина» and «Рукавичка» can form a separate family-support series, with distinct purposes: **community care for a child lacking support** versus **warmth and togetherness in a large family**. Avoid implying military service, a particular profession or family circumstance automatically establishes legal eligibility. Do not equate children in one-parent households with orphans or children deprived of parental care; they are distinct groups whose inclusion/criteria require separate review.

**Open editorial question:** Confirm whether «Дякую за освіту» was meant for educators, or as an umbrella name for the mentioned service professions (firefighters, electricians and possibly other professions). Keep the originally proposed title visible rather than silently reassigning it.

## Open decisions and constraints

Define funding pool, legal eligibility, child rights, appropriate accessible application UX, non-discriminatory verification and consent; review whether the mandatory response requirement creates legal or access barriers.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
