# Referral Rewards & Subscription Credits

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** growth

## Goal

Explore a recommendation mechanism tied to paid subscriptions.

## Intended scope

- Offer a month of subscription credit when an invited person purchases eligible subscription (user-proposed economics).
- Provide a clear parent-facing offer, particularly to mothers/caregivers; avoid misleading 'free' claims.
- Include fraud controls, refund/clawback policy, personal-data privacy and consent.

## Open decisions and constraints

Resolve free-learning mission vs subscription products, regional consumer law, attribution and marketing economics.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
