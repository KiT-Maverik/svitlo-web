# Lesson Verification & Editorial Provenance

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** growth

## Goal

Expose how a Lesson's educational accuracy was reviewed.

## Intended scope

- Distinguish AI-assisted draft, editorially reviewed, verified by named qualified subject expert, and other honest validation states.
- Show source/reference and date/version of verification, consent to public attribution, and review expiration where relevant.
- Do not conflate CI passing with scientific verification or give a misleading seal of approval.

## Open decisions and constraints

Design source-of-truth record, reviewer accountability and public badge policy.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
