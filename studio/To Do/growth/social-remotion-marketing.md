# Programmable Social Video Marketing Project

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** growth

## Goal

Use Remotion to produce maintainable localized video campaigns.

## Intended scope

- Support reproducible Full HD (1920×1080) and Story (1080×1920), variants, captions, programmatic data and reviewed media.
- Consider a distinct marketing package/project; if media/assets become very large, extract it to separate repository while retaining versioned configs and clean linkage.
- Do not force marketing video assets into each textbook runtime package.

## Open decisions and constraints

Decide ownership, render infrastructure, storage costs, rights and package extraction.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
