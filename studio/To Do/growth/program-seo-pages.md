# Indexable Program, Course & Microcourse Pages

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** growth

## Goal

Allow each Program to become a searchable first-class content entity.

## Intended scope

- Generate dedicated pages and metadata for Program/course/microcourse and targeted search intents.
- Publish clear descriptions, lesson sequences and structured metadata, with approved claims only.
- Keep source definitions versioned, localized and canonical; avoid duplicate thin content.

## Open decisions and constraints

Design static generation, route slugs, canonical URLs, search indexing and Program contract.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
