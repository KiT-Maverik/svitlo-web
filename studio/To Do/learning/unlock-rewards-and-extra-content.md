# Optional Content for Unlocking Remaining Notes

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** learning

## Goal

Explore voluntary learning incentives around unlockable Notes.

## Intended scope

- Offer access to extra practice or non-canonical materials when appropriate to unlock remaining Notes.
- Don't confuse locked Notes with missing mandatory educational content; extra incentives must not become a coercive or unfair gate.
- Preserve clear learner choice, focus and accessibility.

## Open decisions and constraints

Decide whether gamification adds value, whether rewards bias comprehension, and consent/parental controls.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
