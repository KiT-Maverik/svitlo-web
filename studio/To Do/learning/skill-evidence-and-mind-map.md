# Skill Evidence Persistence & Learner Competency Map

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** learning

## Goal

Track observed evidence from Lesson/Quiz attempts to display skill progress.

## Intended scope

- Persist learner attempts/results with links to actual question, Skill, assessment method, Lesson and Program stable IDs.
- Produce mind map of skills under a curriculum or Program with evidence-based, uncertainty-aware progress indicators.
- Show what has been practiced, where errors occur, last missed questions and what may need more work.
- Support a toggle for empty/uncovered Skills because a Program/Curriculum may omit catalog entries.
- Keep mastery distinct from Lesson completion and a single successful answer.

## Open decisions and constraints

Design Skill evidence semantics with Assessment Architect; privacy, retention, consent, offline/sync.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
