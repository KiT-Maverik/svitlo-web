# Learning How to Learn Course

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** learning

## Goal

Teach learners and families how Svitlo's learning techniques work.

## Intended scope

- Explain retrieval practice, spacing, active feedback, practice, Notes and sensible workload.
- Give short interactive demonstrations rather than just describing the methods.
- Adapt language to children and to parents/caregivers, especially mothers who may guide younger learners.
- Avoid exaggerated 'biohacking' promises and make sources available.

## Open decisions and constraints

Define age groups and audience-specific variants.
