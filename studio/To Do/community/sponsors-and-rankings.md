# Sponsor Recognition and Relative Tiers

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** community

## Goal

Allow sponsoring the platform or an individual Lesson/Program.

## Intended scope

- Consider Platinum/Gold/Silver tiers based on relative contribution/rank rather than fixed price, as user-proposed model.
- Track sponsors and contributions with transparent eligibility windows and conflict-of-interest protections.
- Distinguish charitable donation, sponsorship service, and paid ad placement as legal/business decisions.

## Open decisions and constraints

Choose tier algorithm, disclosure obligations, revenue and nonprofit constraints.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
