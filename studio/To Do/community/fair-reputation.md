# Non-Toxic Reputation and Anti-Farming

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** community

## Goal

Explore constructive recognition of genuine learning without compulsive leaderboards.

## Intended scope

- Reward meaningful verified learning actions or community help cautiously; resist repeated-answer point farming and overnight grinding.
- Cap repeatable earning per question/activity or detect diminishing value; avoid unfair advantage to faster or more connected devices.
- Design optional inclusive/private modes, no public child-level exposure by default and no shame ranking.
- Separate points from actual Skill mastery.

## Open decisions and constraints

Validate motivational effects, fraud/farming limits, family controls and ranking fairness.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
