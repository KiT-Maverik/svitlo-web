# Sponsor Thank-You Prompts & Preferences

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** community

## Goal

Show nonintrusive recognition when a Lesson exists thanks to a sponsor.

## Intended scope

- Offer sponsor attribution with optional verified external website link and safe link disclosure.
- Allow temporary suppression (one day, 2–3 days, etc.) or never show this prompt again.
- Keep acknowledgements clearly differentiated from instruction and not condition access to mandatory content.
- Respect offline visibility, localization, minor protection and UX accessibility.

## Open decisions and constraints

Decide frequency cap, sponsor conflicts, persistence and parental controls.

**Implementation note:** Backlog only, not an approved new runtime contract or claim of functionality.
