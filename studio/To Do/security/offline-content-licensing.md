# Offline Licensing, Distribution Rights & Abuse Resistance

**Status:** Proposed — not implemented by this document.
**Area:** security

## Goal

Design a practical licensing/entitlement strategy compatible with genuine offline learning.

## Intended scope

- Define which resources are free/openly shareable versus restricted, and establish actual content copyrights/third-party permissions.
- Support intended offline operation without requiring perpetual online license checks.
- Discourage casual copying where lawful/practical; acknowledge that **a fully offline client cannot guarantee uncopyable content or absolute DRM security**.
- Avoid locking learners out of previously downloaded educational content unexpectedly.

## Open decisions and constraints

Choose licenses, business model and scope of deterrence; evaluate user privacy and recoverability.

**Implementation note:** This is a product-backlog specification, not approval to edit runtime contracts, add dependencies or claim the feature exists.
