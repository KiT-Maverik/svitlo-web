# Psychology — Skills Taxonomy

Date: 2026-10-29

Psychology is the empirical study of behavior, experience, and mental processes across research methods, biology, perception, cognition, motivation, emotion, development, relationships, individual differences, wellbeing, and applied contexts. This taxonomy is independent of age, course sequence, expected mastery depth, and professional licensing. Philosophy evaluates arguments and normative claims; biology studies detailed anatomy and physiology; health and safety covers practical medical support; psychology focuses on how people actually think, feel, learn, and behave. Education is not a clinical assessment, diagnosis, or psychological intervention, and no learner is required to disclose private experiences.

Breadth references, not imposed curricula: [APA Guidelines 3.0](https://www.apa.org/about/policy/undergraduate-psychology-major) and [OpenStax Psychology 2e](https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/preface).
