# Biology editorial documents

The [Biology Skills Taxonomy](skills/taxonomy.md) is an **unapproved editorial draft** containing 11 proposed Domains, 26 Groups and 147 candidate Skills, with outstanding editor feedback and cross-subject decisions recorded in [PR #49](https://github.com/KiT-Maverik/svitlo-web/pull/49). The document belongs in this textbook package as the durable source of the proposal.

The dated [Skill Taxonomy Review](history/skill-taxonomy-review.md) preserves **editor-approved decisions, their reasoning and superseded proposals** from October 28–29, 2026. It is historical context, **not** a second canonical taxonomy or a list of pending instructions. The six source-backed teaching scenarios are maintained in [Studio Subject Proposition](../../../studio/Subject%20Proposition.md).

No per-entity Definition documents are expected: the taxonomy itself is the canonical pedagogical source. Taxonomy code, UUIDs, curricula and lessons have **not** been created. Review and approve the taxonomy structure and atomicity before progressing to the next workflow.
