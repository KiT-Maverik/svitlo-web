# Chemistry — Skills Taxonomy

Date: 2026-10-29

This is the audience-independent catalog of independently assessable chemistry competencies, not a grade sequence or a required level of formalism. It covers chemical investigation, matter and its structure, composition, mixtures, transformations, quantitative relationships, organic and inorganic chemistry, and chemical applications. A curriculum—not this taxonomy—selects competencies, depth, prerequisites and methods for particular learners.

**Subject boundaries:** Physics covers physical laws beyond their chemistry-specific explanatory use; biology covers organism-level functions and inheritance; engineering covers industrial system and device design; health and safety covers general life-safety behavior and medical advice. Chemistry retains substance-specific risk interpretation and chemical analysis. Questions such as “What is this made of?” can reference existing competencies rather than creating a separate encyclopedia Domain. No hazardous practical procedures are prescribed.

**Breadth references (for gap analysis, not claims of standards alignment):** [American Chemical Society — Core Concepts](https://www.acs.org/education/policies/middle-and-high-school-chemistry/core-concepts.html); [Royal Society of Chemistry — Chemistry Curriculum Framework](https://www.rsc.org/policy-and-campaigning/education/chemistry-curriculum-framework). These curricula are informative evidence, not an age restriction on this taxonomy.
