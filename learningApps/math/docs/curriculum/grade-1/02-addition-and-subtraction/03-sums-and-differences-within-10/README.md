# Sums and Differences Within 10

> **Curriculum: Mathematics — Grade 1 · Addition and Subtraction · Lesson 03**
> **Status:** Draft lesson card; a complete methodological Learning Brief has not yet been written.

## Learning objective

Develop basic addition and subtraction facts within 10.

Use concrete number relationships instead of memorizing isolated answers; subtraction is first shown through removal.
