# Addition and Subtraction

> **Curriculum: Mathematics — Grade 1 · Module 02**
> **Status:** All ten lessons have draft storyboards (60 sections), but full methodological Learning Briefs have not yet been prepared.

The authoritative learning sequence and skill mapping are specified in [Grade 1 Curriculum](../grade-1.curriculum.md). This is a learning module, not a SkillGroup in the taxonomy.

## Learning goal

Develop concrete addition and subtraction through sums and differences within 20. Part–whole models, zero, commutativity, and bridging through ten support understanding of operations. Inverse relationships are introduced as a way to check work.

## Lessons

- 01. [Parts and a Whole](./01-parts-and-a-whole/README.md) — Recognize a whole as two parts and find a missing part. [Storyboard](./01-parts-and-a-whole/Sections/README.md)
- 02. [What Addition Means](./02-what-addition-means/README.md) — Model addition as joining or adding objects, including zero. [Storyboard](./02-what-addition-means/Sections/README.md)
- 03. [Sums and Differences Within 10](./03-sums-and-differences-within-10/README.md) — Develop basic addition and subtraction facts within 10. [Storyboard](./03-sums-and-differences-within-10/Sections/README.md)
- 04. [Number Bonds to 10](./04-number-bonds-to-10/README.md) — Build ten from two complementary parts. [Storyboard](./04-number-bonds-to-10/Sections/README.md)
- 05. [Changing the Order of Addends](./05-changing-the-order-of-addends/README.md) — Explain why swapping addends preserves the sum. [Storyboard](./05-changing-the-order-of-addends/Sections/README.md)
- 06. [Making Ten](./06-making-ten/README.md) — Use making ten as a mental addition strategy. [Storyboard](./06-making-ten/Sections/README.md)
- 07. [Addition Across Ten](./07-addition-across-ten/README.md) — Add one-digit numbers when the sum crosses ten. [Storyboard](./07-addition-across-ten/Sections/README.md)
- 08. [What Subtraction Means](./08-what-subtraction-means/README.md) — Model subtraction, including subtracting zero or all objects. [Storyboard](./08-what-subtraction-means/Sections/README.md)
- 09. [Subtraction Across Ten](./09-subtraction-across-ten/README.md) — Subtract within 20 by passing through ten. [Storyboard](./09-subtraction-across-ten/Sections/README.md)
- 10. [Calculations Within 20](./10-calculations-within-20/README.md) — Apply addition and subtraction within 20 and check inverses. [Storyboard](./10-calculations-within-20/Sections/README.md)

## Pedagogical review required

Lesson 03, **Sums and Differences Within 10**, precedes lesson 08, **What Subtraction Means**. This is a possible prerequisite-sequencing problem. Lesson 03's storyboard treats subtraction only as the concrete action of taking objects away, while formal subtraction notation appears in lesson 08. A methodologist must review the sequence before approving the learning plan or formal assessments.
