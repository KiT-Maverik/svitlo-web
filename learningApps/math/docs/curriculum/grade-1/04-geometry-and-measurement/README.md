# Geometry and Measurement

> **Curriculum: Mathematics — Grade 1 · Module 04**
> **Status:** All eight lessons have draft storyboards (48 sections); full methodological Learning Briefs remain to be written.

The authoritative learning sequence and skill mapping are specified in [Grade 1 Curriculum](../grade-1.curriculum.md). This is a learning module, not a SkillGroup in the taxonomy.

## Learning goal

Recognize shapes, spatial relations, and simple measurement principles. Learners identify two- and three-dimensional figures, compose shapes, compare mass and capacity, and measure or estimate length with appropriate units.

## Lessons

- 01. [Left, Right, and Between](./01-left-right-and-between/README.md) — Describe positions and follow short spatial directions. [Storyboard](./01-left-right-and-between/Sections/README.md)
- 02. [Plane Shapes](./02-plane-shapes/README.md) — Identify common plane shapes despite changes of size or orientation. [Storyboard](./02-plane-shapes/Sections/README.md)
- 03. [Solid Shapes](./03-solid-shapes/README.md) — Recognize common three-dimensional solids. [Storyboard](./03-solid-shapes/Sections/README.md)
- 04. [Composing Shapes](./04-composing-shapes/README.md) — Compose and decompose shapes into familiar parts. [Storyboard](./04-composing-shapes/Sections/README.md)
- 05. [Longer, Shorter, and Equal Length](./05-longer-shorter-and-equal-length/README.md) — Compare lengths and measure them with repeated equal units. [Storyboard](./05-longer-shorter-and-equal-length/Sections/README.md)
- 06. [Rulers and Centimeters](./06-rulers-and-centimeters/README.md) — Measure from a ruler's zero mark and draw a given length. [Storyboard](./06-rulers-and-centimeters/Sections/README.md)
- 07. [Heavier and Greater Capacity](./07-heavier-and-greater-capacity/README.md) — Compare mass and capacity using appropriate models. [Storyboard](./07-heavier-and-greater-capacity/Sections/README.md)
- 08. [Estimating Length Before Measuring](./08-estimating-length-before-measuring/README.md) — Estimate length, then test the estimate by measuring. [Storyboard](./08-estimating-length-before-measuring/Sections/README.md)

## Pedagogical review required

Methodological review must distinguish physical centimeters from uncalibrated screen pixels. Comparing mass and comparing capacity are also different concepts and must remain distinct in the learning materials.
