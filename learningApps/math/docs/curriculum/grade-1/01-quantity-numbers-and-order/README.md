## Learning goal

Establish cardinality, number notation, counting order, and grouping within 20. Learners move from counting individual objects to comparing and ordering written numbers. Equal groups and the first ten provide the foundations for later arithmetic.

## Pedagogical scope

The module develops counting and number sense within 0–20, including symbolic notation, numerical order, comparison, number lines, and groups of ten. Learners progress from observable collections to numeric representations.

**Prerequisites:** No prior formal mathematics is required; an emerging reader must still be able to participate.

**Teaching principle:** Establish meaning through concrete actions before formal symbolism. Do not treat a single activity as evidence that multiple distinct Skills have been mastered.

This module excludes multiplication, the formal arithmetic operations taught in the following module, and place-value notation extending to 100. Assessment criteria belong to the Assessment Architect rather than to these module briefs.

For lessons 01, 02, and 03, the storyboard section counts are 6, 8, and 7 respectively; each of the other eight lessons has six sections.
